# Customer Support FAQ - General Help Guide

## Quick Answers to Common Questions

### Getting Started
**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click "Sign Up" on the homepage, enter your email and create a password. You'll receive a verification email - click the link to activate your account. Alternatively, you can sign up using Google, Microsoft, or Apple ID for faster registration.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered email address. You'll receive a password reset link valid for 24 hours. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within 5 minutes.

**Q: Can I change my email address after registration?**
A: Yes. Go to Account Settings → Profile → Edit Email. You'll need to verify the new email address before it becomes active. Your old email will remain active until verification is complete.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Yes! Download our app from the App Store (iOS 14+) or Google Play Store (Android 10+). The app offers full functionality including push notifications, offline mode for saved content, and biometric login.

### Account Management
**Q: How do I update my profile information?**
A: Log in → Click your avatar/name → Account Settings → Profile. Update name, phone, address, preferences. Changes save automatically.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?**
A: Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication → Enable. Choose SMS or authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator). Save backup codes in a secure location.

**Q: I'm locked out of my account. What do I do?**
A: After 5 failed login attempts, accounts lock for 30 minutes. Wait or use "Forgot Password." If 2FA is enabled and you lost access, use backup codes or contact support with government-issued ID for manual verification (24-48 hour process).

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Account Settings → Security → Delete Account. This is irreversible. All data is purged within 30 days per our data retention policy. Active subscriptions must be cancelled first.

### Billing & Subscriptions
**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Bank transfer (annual plans only), Purchase orders (Enterprise plans).

**Q: How do I update my payment method?**
A: Account Settings → Billing → Payment Methods → Add/Replace. Set a default. Old methods can be kept for reference or removed.

**Q: Can I get an invoice for my payments?**
A: Yes. Account Settings → Billing → Invoice History. Download PDF invoices for all past payments. Invoices include VAT/GST where applicable.

**Q: What's your refund policy?**
A: Monthly plans: 7-day money-back guarantee. Annual plans: 30-day money-back guarantee. Enterprise: Custom terms per contract. Refunds processed to original payment method within 5-10 business days.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Account Settings → Billing → Subscription → Cancel. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds for unused time unless within money-back window.

### Technical Issues
**Q: The website/app isn't loading. What should I check?**
A: 1) Check internet connection. 2) Clear browser cache/cookies or app cache. 3) Try incognito/private mode. 4) Disable browser extensions. 5) Try a different browser/device. 6) Check our status page at status.company.com for outages.

**Q: I'm not receiving emails from you.**
A: Check spam/junk/promotions folders. Add noreply@company.com and support@company.com to contacts. Corporate emails may block automated messages - contact your IT admin. Resend verification from Account Settings.

**Q: File uploads keep failing.**
A: Max file size: 100MB (Free), 2GB (Pro), 10GB (Enterprise). Supported formats: PDF, DOC, XLS, PPT, JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV, ZIP. Check file isn't corrupted or password-protected.

**Q: How do I clear my cache/cookies?**
A: Chrome: Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Delete → All time → Cached images, Cookies. Firefox: Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Everything. Safari: Develop → Empty Caches. Mobile app: Settings → Clear Cache.

### Privacy & Security
**Q: How is my data protected?**
A: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. SOC 2 Type II certified. GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA compliant where applicable. Annual third-party penetration testing. Bug bounty program active.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes. Account Settings → Privacy → Export Data. Generates ZIP with JSON/CSV of all your data within 24 hours. Download link expires in 7 days.

**Q: Do you sell my data?**
A: Never. We don't sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing. Data is only shared with subprocessors for service delivery (payment processors, cloud hosting, email delivery) under strict DPAs.

**Q: How do I report a security vulnerability?**
A: Email security@company.com or use our responsible disclosure portal at security.company.com. We respond within 48 hours. Eligible findings qualify for bug bounty rewards ($100-$10,000).
